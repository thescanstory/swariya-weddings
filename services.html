Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a luxury wedding planner cost in Bengaluru? −

Wedding planner fees in Bengaluru typically range from 5-10% of the total wedding budget for full planning services, or a transparent flat fee for defined scopes. Swariya Weddings offers customized quotes based on guest count, multi-day ceremony needs, and venue choice. See our detailed Bengaluru Wedding Cost Guide 2026 for itemized price breakdowns.

What is the difference between full planning and day-of coordination? +

Full wedding planning is end-to-end management spanning 6–12 months: venue selection, design concepts, vendor contracts, guest logistics, and budget audits. Day-of coordination is on-site execution during the event days for couples who have booked their own vendors but need a professional team to manage cue sheets, timelines, and crisis management.

Do you offer packages for intimate weddings and micro-weddings in Bengaluru? +

Yes. Swariya Weddings curates custom intimate wedding packages for 50–150 guests at boutique heritage venues and garden resorts, providing focused décor design, catering management, and photography without unnecessary overhead.

How far in advance should we book Swariya Weddings? +

We recommend booking 8–12 months in advance, especially for high-demand wedding muhurtham dates between October and February. Premium Bengaluru venues and vendors book quickly during peak wedding seasons.

Does Swariya charge vendor commissions or markups? +

No. Swariya Weddings works strictly on transparent management fees. We pass 100% of vendor trade discounts and direct negotiated rates to our couples, ensuring unbiased vendor recommendations.
