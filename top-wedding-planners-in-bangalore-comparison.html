✦ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Top Luxury Wedding Planners in Bangalore Comparison Guide — FAQs

What is the difference between commission-based planners and fixed-fee planners?

Commission-based planners take hidden 15-30% cuts from vendors. Swariya operates on a fixed professional management fee with 100% direct vendor billing and 0% markups.

Why is 3D architectural decor visualization important?

It ensures you see exact photorealistic renders of your mandap, stage, and lighting before committing lakhs to physical fabrication.

How much advance notice is required to book top Bangalore wedding venues?

Peak muhurtham dates (November through February) require 9 to 12 months advance booking for prime Bangalore properties like The Tamarind Tree, Palace Grounds, and Taj West End.

Can a wedding planner help reduce venue and catering costs in Bangalore?

Yes. Flat-fee fiduciary planners negotiate wholesale corporate rates directly with five-star properties and catering partners, passing 100% of volume discounts directly to the client without pocketing broker cuts.

What questions should you ask before hiring a wedding planner?

Ask for their on-ground team size on wedding day, crisis contingency protocols, direct vendor pricing access, and client references.
