✦ CLIENT DATA INTEGRITY

Privacy Policy & Confidentiality Terms

Your personal event data, vendor contracts, guest lists, and budget details are strictly confidential.

1. Introduction & Fiduciary Commitment

At Swariya Weddings, we are committed to protecting the privacy, financial details, and personal event information of every couple and family we serve. As a luxury planning atelier operating with zero vendor commissions, our fiduciary duty is solely to our clients.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to plan and coordinate your wedding celebration:

  • Contact Information: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, and home/international addresses.
  • Event Parameters: Target dates, guest counts, regional cultural traditions, aesthetic preferences, and budget limits.
  • Logistics Data: Guest flight schedules and dietary preferences submitted for hospitality coordination.

3. Zero Data Monetization Guarantee

We never sell, lease, or distribute your contact details or event briefs to commercial telemarketers, lead aggregators, or unauthorized third parties. Vendor contact sharing is conducted solely with your explicit approval for direct service contracts.

4. Digital Workspace & Client Portal Security

All planning worksheets, run-sheets, and invoice repositories inside the Swariya Wedding OS workspace are encrypted and restricted to authorized client leads and our core executive planning team.
