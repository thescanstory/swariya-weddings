Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Swariya Weddings? −

Swariya Weddings is a Pan-India luxury wedding planning company founded in 2020 and headquartered in Bengaluru. With 150+ weddings planned, 500+ happy clients, and a 4.9/5 rating, Swariya provides end-to-end bespoke wedding planning, architectural décor, vendor curation, and on-ground logistics across Goa, Rajasthan, Kerala, Bengaluru, Coorg, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and nationwide.

Where is Swariya Weddings headquartered? +

Swariya Weddings is headquartered at #343, 9th Main, 22nd Cross Rd, 7th Sector, HSR Layout, Bengaluru 560102, with active on-ground wedding execution teams dispatched across Goa, Rajasthan, Kerala, Karnataka, and all major Indian destinations. You can schedule an in-person design consultation at our studio or connect virtually via Google Meet or WhatsApp (+91 80505 73382).

How does Swariya's zero-markup pricing work? +

We charge a transparent flat management fee for our planning and coordination services. All third-party vendor costs (caterers, photographers, venues, makeup artists) are contracted directly between you and the vendor at wholesale rates with zero hidden commission.

Can Swariya Weddings coordinate destination weddings across India? +

Yes, Swariya Weddings regularly coordinates destination weddings across India (Goa, Coorg, Chikmagalur, Kerala, Udaipur, and Jaipur) and internationally, managing guest hospitality, travel logistics, vendor setup, and multi-day ceremonies.

How does Swariya work with NRI couples planning a wedding in India? +

For couples living in the US, UK, Middle East, or Singapore, Swariya provides time-zone aligned virtual consultations, digital 3D décor renders, transparent shared budget ledgers, and end-to-end guest accommodation management.
