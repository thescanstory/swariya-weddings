### Template: Helpful Value-First Response (Zero Spam)
> *"For Bangalore weddings, your major cost driver will be venue rental + mandatory in-house catering vs hiring an open lawn that allows outside vendors.
> 
> A few quick pointers from recent 2026 budget benchmarks:
