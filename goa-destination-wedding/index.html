What decides the cost of a Goa wedding?

Four line items make up most of a Goa wedding budget: venue and rooms, food and bar, décor and production, and guest logistics (flights, airport transfers, welcome kits). Photography, entertainment, makeup and planning fees make up the rest.

Guest count matters more than anything else, because in Goa you are usually paying for guest rooms for two or three nights, not just a banquet hall for one evening. Moving from 150 to 300 guests roughly doubles rooms, meals and transfers, while décor rises much more slowly.

Your choice of venue sets the baseline. A private heritage villa with a Portuguese courtyard suits an intimate 50–150 guest wedding. Large beach resorts in North and South Goa can room 300+ guests on one property, which keeps logistics simple but raises the per-room rate.

When is the best time for a destination wedding in Goa?

The peak season runs from October to February, when the weather is dry and sunsets are reliable for beach mandaps. It is also when resorts charge their highest rates and the best dates sell out 9–12 months ahead.

March and April are warmer but noticeably cheaper, and work well for evening functions. The monsoon (June to September) brings the lowest prices but makes outdoor beach ceremonies risky, so plan indoor or covered backups if you choose it.

  • Oct–Feb: best weather, highest rates, book early
  • Mar–Apr: warmer, better value, schedule events after sunset
  • Jun–Sep: lowest rates, needs covered venues

Beach, villa or five-star resort: which Goa venue fits your budget?

Beach resorts give you the sunset mandap most couples imagine, plus rooms for everyone on one property. They are the easiest to run for 200+ guests.

Heritage villas in North Goa offer Portuguese architecture, courtyards and pool-side sundowner Sangeets. They are ideal for 50–150 guests and a more personal, house-party feel.

Five-star properties in South Goa offer the calmest beaches, polished service and in-house catering, and usually the highest per-plate cost.

What is the cheapest month for a wedding in Goa?

The monsoon months (June to September) have the lowest resort rates, followed by March and April. October to February is the most expensive because the weather is best and demand is highest.

How far in advance should we book a Goa wedding venue?

Book 9–12 months ahead for peak-season dates between October and February, especially for beach resorts that can room 300+ guests. Off-season dates can often be secured 4–6 months out.

Can we have a beach mandap in Goa?

Yes. Many North and South Goa resorts have private beach lawns for sunset ceremonies. We plan a covered backup for every outdoor function and schedule loud music to end by 10 pm, in line with India's noise rules.
