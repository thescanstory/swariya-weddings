✦ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Goa Destination Wedding FAQs

How much does a destination wedding in Goa cost in 2026?

A luxury 2-day destination wedding in Goa for 150–200 guests typically ranges from ₹45 Lakhs to ₹1.2 Crores. This covers 5-star beachfront resort rooms/buyouts, curated gourmet catering, custom coastal floral mandaps, DJ sound licenses, and full-service coordination. With Swariya's zero-commission fiduciary pricing, couples avoid typical 15–20% vendor kickbacks.

What is the best time of the year for a beach wedding in Goa?

The ideal wedding season in Goa is from late October through early April, offering balmy ocean breezes, golden sunsets, and minimal rain. November to February is peak season with high resort demand, while late October and March offer exceptional value for luxury resort buyouts.

Can Swariya coordinate weddings in Goa if we live in Bangalore or abroad?

Yes. Over 60% of our couples are NRIs or metropolitan professionals based in Bangalore, Mumbai, Singapore, the US, and the UK. Our online Wedding OS client portal gives you real-time tracking of budgets, guest RSVPs, vendor contracts, and 3D decor designs from anywhere in the world.
