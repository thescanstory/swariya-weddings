Amita Rasa Wedding Details & Guidelines

What makes Amita Rasa unique among Bengaluru wedding venues? −

Amita Rasa features traditional South Indian Dravidian stone temple-style architecture framed against the dramatic backdrop of Nandi Hills. The central stepped Kalyani and open-air amphitheatre create natural stadium-style seating for guests to view varmala and muhurtham rites.

What are the accommodation options for guests? +

Amita Rasa features on-site bridal suites and private rooms. For guest contingents of 150–300 staying guests, Swariya Weddings arranges luxury resort blocks at nearby properties (JW Marriott Prestige Golfshire, Mulberry Shades) with scheduled shuttles.
