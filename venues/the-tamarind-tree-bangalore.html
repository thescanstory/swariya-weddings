Everything You Need to Know About Weddings at The Tamarind Tree

What is the guest capacity at The Tamarind Tree Bangalore? −

The Tamarind Tree can comfortably accommodate between 150 and 1,000 guests. The property features multiple interconnected spaces: an open-air amphitheatre for intimate varmala ceremonies, a central courtyard, pond pavilion for muhurtham mandaps, and extensive banquet lawns for dinner receptions.

Does The Tamarind Tree allow outside caterers and decorators? +

Yes. The Tamarind Tree allows outside catering vendors, making it ideal for authentic South Indian traditional meals (Sadya / Oota on banana leaves) as well as premium North Indian and continental banquets. Outside decorators must be approved or empanelled; Swariya Weddings has executed multiple luxury weddings on this estate.

How many guest rooms are available on property? +

The estate provides 28 heritage rooms and suites with vintage antique woodwork and stone architecture, accommodating roughly 60–75 staying guests. For larger guest lists, nearby luxury hotels on Kanakapura Road and Bannerghatta Road can be booked with Swariya's shuttle coordination.

How far in advance should I book The Tamarind Tree? +

Prime weekend muhurtham dates between October and February book 10 to 14 months in advance. Weekday or off-season dates can sometimes be secured 4 to 6 months in advance. Contact Swariya Weddings to verify available dates and negotiate rental packages.

How does Swariya Weddings manage weddings at The Tamarind Tree? +

Swariya handles end-to-end estate production: heritage-themed floral mandap installations, warm ambient fairy and brass lamp lighting, power generator management, guest shuttle logistics, sound licensing, and catering kitchen setup.
