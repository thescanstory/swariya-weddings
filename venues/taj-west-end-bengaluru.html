Planning a Wedding at Taj West End Bengaluru

What are the best event spaces at Taj West End? −

The Prince of Wales Lawn is Bengaluru's premier garden venue for 400–800 guests, bordered by 150-year-old banyan trees and flora. For indoor receptions, the Grand Ballroom holds 250 seated guests, while the Blue Verandah and Mynt Lawns are ideal for mehendi and morning haldi rituals.

How does Swariya Weddings style Taj West End lawns? +

Swariya creates botanical and heritage mandaps that complement the century-old trees—utilizing warm fairy-lit tree wraps, brass urlis, hanging mogra chandeliers, and low-profile raised stages that protect the estate lawns.

What are the noise and sound cutoff timings? +

Outdoor music on the Prince of Wales Lawn adheres to city sound regulations with acoustic levels monitored after 10:00 PM. Indoor ballrooms can continue celebrations and late-night afterparties.
