Frequently Asked Questions About The Leela Palace Weddings

What is the guest capacity at The Leela Palace Bengaluru? −

The Leela Palace Bengaluru offers multiple banquet halls. The Grand Ballroom accommodates up to 500 seated and 650 floating guests for grand receptions. The Royal Ballroom and outdoor terraces provide an intimate setting for sangeet or engagement functions of 100 to 250 guests.

What are the catering packages and per-plate costs? +

Food and beverages are provided exclusively by The Leela Palace's award-winning culinary team. Per-plate menus range from ₹3,800 to ₹6,500+ plus taxes, featuring curated regional Indian, Asian, and Mediterranean menus with live live gourmet counters.

Does the hotel allow outside wedding decorators? +

Yes. Swariya Weddings coordinates directly with The Leela Palace banquet and security teams for loading dock access, sound limitations, and fire-safety compliant ballroom transforms including crystal chandelier ceiling treatments and stage structures.

How far in advance should I book The Leela Palace for a wedding? +

We recommend reserving dates 8 to 12 months in advance, especially for weekend dates in the winter wedding season (November to February). Swariya Weddings assists couples with group room block rates and banquet minimum spend negotiations.
