Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highlight of Royalton Leisure? +

The natural granite rock formations provide a breathtaking, majestic backdrop for evening pheras, cocktail receptions, and pre-wedding shoots.

Are fireworks and special effects permitted? +

Cold pyros, dry ice fog, and controlled low-smoke effects are permitted with prior clearance managed by Swariya Weddings.

What is Swariya Weddings' coordination package for Royalton Leisure? +

Swariya provides full spatial design, lighting across the granite formations, power backup management, catering oversight, and guest flow coordination.
