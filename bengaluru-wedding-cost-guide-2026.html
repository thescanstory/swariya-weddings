Bengaluru Wedding Cost Guide 2026

An itemized, empirical cost analysis for weddings in Bengaluru, Mysuru, and Hyderabad across guest counts, venue categories, catering, décor production, and photography.

Currency:
Methodology & Data Source

This guide is compiled from real financial ledgers across 150+ weddings planned by Swariya Weddings in Bengaluru, Mysuru, and Hyderabad between 2020 and 2026. All figures reflect verified vendor trade rates with zero hidden markups.

1. Estimated Total Budget by Guest Scale

Indicative total budgets for a 2-day wedding celebration (Mehendi/Sangeet + Muhurtham & Reception):

Wedding Scale Guest Count Venue Tier Estimated Total Budget Band
Intimate / Micro 50 – 150 guests Boutique Garden / Heritage Villa ₹12 Lakhs – ₹25 Lakhs
Mid-Sized Luxury 150 – 400 guests Heritage Estate / 4-Star Resort ₹25 Lakhs – ₹65 Lakhs
Grand Celebration 400 – 800 guests 5-Star Ballroom / Premium Lawn ₹65 Lakhs – ₹1.2 Crores
Royal / High-Scale 800 – 1,500+ guests Palace Grounds / 5-Star Hotel ₹1.2 Crores – ₹2.5 Crores+
