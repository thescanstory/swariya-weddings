What are the best months for a wedding in Udaipur?

The ideal wedding season in Udaipur is October through March, when the weather is pleasant with cool evenings perfect for outdoor lakeside celebrations. Monsoon months (July–September) offer lush green hills at attractive resort rates.

How do guest boat transfers work for island palace weddings?

For island venues like Jagmandir, private jetty boats ferry guests across Lake Pichola. Our hospitality team coordinates scheduled departures, safety vests, welcome drinks, and baggage handling.

Can we bring our own catering chefs to Udaipur venues?

Many standalone heritage venues allow specialized Maharaj or outside catering teams for community-specific menus (such as Marwari, Gujarati, Jain, or South Indian cuisines), while luxury hotels provide custom in-house culinary curation.

How far in advance should we book an Udaipur wedding palace?

Prime auspicious dates between November and February book out 9 to 14 months in advance. We recommend locking down venue contracts as early as possible.
