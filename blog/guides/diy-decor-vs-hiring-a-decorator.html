DIY Décor vs. Hiring a Decorator

A real cost and effort comparison between DIY wedding décor and hiring a professional decorator.

A seaside wedding tent lit with chandeliers — from a Swariya-planned wedding

DIY décor looks like an obvious way to save money in a Pinterest board, and sometimes it is — but the real cost comparison includes labor, time, and the risk of it not coming together the way it looked online.

Where DIY genuinely works

Small, personal touches — table numbers, welcome signs, favor packaging — scale well as DIY projects because the stakes of one imperfect piece are low and the cost savings are real and immediate.

Where DIY gets expensive fast

Large-scale elements — mandap structures, floral installations, lighting design — usually require rented equipment, specialized labor, and setup expertise that erode the savings once you price it honestly, and a failed large installation is much harder to recover from on the day itself.

A middle path worth considering

Hiring a decorator for structural and large-scale elements while handling small personal touches yourself — this is what most of our own clients actually land on rather than choosing one extreme.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DIY decor actually cheaper once labor is counted?

For small elements, usually yes. For large installations (mandaps, elaborate floral walls), often not by much once you account for rented equipment and the hours involved — professional decorators have existing inventory and setup efficiency that's hard to replicate.

What's the biggest risk of DIY decor?

Time — décor setup usually happens in a compressed window right before the event, and DIY elements that aren't ready on schedule become a day-of crisis rather than a manageable delay.
