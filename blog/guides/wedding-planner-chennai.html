Luxury Wedding Planner in Chennai

From sunset beach mandaps along the East Coast Road (ECR) to opulent seaside palaces in MRC Nagar and timeless heritage mantapams — how to plan a serene, culturally authentic wedding in Chennai.

Sunset coastal mandap — planned & coordinated by Swariya Weddings

Chennai offers a rare wedding charm: the gentle roar of the Bay of Bengal along the scenic East Coast Road combined with deep Carnatic traditions, intricate temple-inspired floral art, and sumptuous South Indian feasts. Whether you're planning a multi-day beach wedding in Mahabalipuram or a grand traditional reception in Nungambakkam, here is our planner's playbook for Chennai.

1. Top Wedding Destinations & Venues in Chennai

Chennai's venues cater to two distinct luxury wedding aesthetics:

  • ECR & Mahabalipuram Beach Resorts: InterContinental Chennai Mahabalipuram and Taj Fisherman's Cove offer private beachfront lawns, golden sunset horizons, and intimate luxury villas ideal for destination-style Sangeets and beachside Pheras.
  • Seaside 5-Star City Ballrooms: The Leela Palace Chennai (MRC Nagar) offers palatial Chettinad architecture overlooking the sea with ballrooms accommodating up to 1,200 guests.
  • Central Luxury Heritage Hotels: Taj Coromandel and ITC Grand Chola (Guindy) deliver signature luxury with world-renowned banquet hospitality and authentic culinary masters.
  • Traditional Heritage Mantapams: Iconic heritage halls like Mayor Ramanathan Chettiar Hall (MRC) and bespoke open-air garden venues in ECR for traditional Muhurthams.
"A coastal wedding along Chennai's ECR requires specialized production — wind-anchored floral trusses, moisture-resistant acoustic systems, and sunset-timed muhurthams."

3. Cultural Rituals & Gastronomic Excellence

We honor authentic South Indian rituals while ensuring modern comfort:

  • Tamil Brahmin & Chettinad Customs: Sumangali Prarthanai, Vratham, Oonjal swing ceremony with traditional Carnatic vocalists, Kasi Yatra, and Muhurtham.
  • Authentic Banana-Leaf Elai Sappadu: Sourcing master caterers from Madurai, Kumbakonam, and Karaikudi for authentic rasam varieties, kootu, payasam, and artisanal filter coffee stations.
  • Contemporary Coastal Sangeet: Modern soundscapes, bespoke cocktail bars, and coastal seafood / global fusion counters for evening celebrations.
