Hiring Vendors Separately vs. a Full Planner

Comparing the DIY-vendor-coordination approach against hiring one full-service planner to manage everything.

A wedding ceremony held at sunset — from a Swariya-planned wedding

This sits one level more specific than the broader planner-vs-DIY question — even couples who want to be hands-on with individual vendor choices sometimes still want a coordinator tying it together.

Hiring vendors separately

Gives you direct relationships and full control over each choice, but means you personally become the coordination layer between vendors who don't necessarily talk to each other, which can create timeline conflicts that only surface close to the day.

A full planner

One accountable party who's already coordinated these same vendor types together before and knows where the typical friction points are — the value isn't just individual vendor selection, it's making sure they work together on the day itself.

A hybrid that works for many couples

Choosing vendors yourself for the pieces you care most about personally (often photography or the wedding outfit), while handing overall coordination and the remaining vendors to a planner.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I hire my own photographer and still use a wedding planner for everything else?

Yes — most planners are happy to coordinate a vendor you've independently chosen, folding them into the overall timeline alongside the vendors they manage directly.

What's the main risk of coordinating vendors yourself?

Timeline conflicts between vendors who aren't used to working together, which tend to surface as day-of scheduling problems rather than being caught in advance.
