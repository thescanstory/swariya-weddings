Questions to Ask a Wedding Planner Before Hiring

The questions worth asking any wedding planner before signing on, to make sure they're the right fit for your wedding.

A stone temple-style mandap dressed in garlands — from a Swariya-planned wedding

A good first consultation should feel like a real conversation, not a sales pitch — these are the questions that tend to reveal the most about whether a planner is the right fit.

About their process

  • How many weddings do you typically take on around the same date?
  • Who will actually be on-site managing our wedding day — you, or a team member we haven't met?
  • What's included in your fee, and what counts as an extra cost?

About their experience

  • Have you planned a wedding of our approximate size and style before?
  • Can we see a full portfolio, not just highlight photos?
  • Can we speak to a recent couple you've worked with?

About the practical details

  • What's your payment schedule and cancellation policy?
  • How do you handle a vendor issue that comes up close to the wedding date?
  • What's your backup plan if something goes wrong on the day itself?

Frequently Asked Questions

Should a wedding planner be willing to share client references?

Yes — a planner confident in their work should have no hesitation connecting you with a recent couple, even if it's an informal conversation rather than a written reference.

Is it a red flag if a planner is vague about their fee structure?

Generally yes — a planner should be able to clearly explain what's included in their base fee and what triggers additional costs, in writing, before you sign anything.
