Weekday vs. Weekend Wedding: Cost Differences

How much you can actually save by choosing a weekday wedding date, and what tradeoffs come with it.

An illuminated palace venue at dusk — from a Swariya-planned wedding

Weekday weddings are one of the most underused cost levers available to couples with any date flexibility — the savings are often larger than people assume.

The savings side

Weekday venue rates commonly run noticeably lower than weekend rates for the same property, and vendors are more available and often more flexible on pricing when they're not competing across multiple Saturday bookings.

The tradeoff side

Guest attendance, particularly for working guests, typically drops for a weekday event — worth weighing honestly against the savings if a large, complete guest list matters more to you than cost.

Where weekday dates work especially well

Smaller, more intimate guest lists where attendance flexibility matters less, or when auspicious-date considerations already point to a weekday regardless of preference.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can you actually save with a weekday wedding?

It varies by venue, but weekday discounts of 15-30% off comparable weekend rates are common across Bengaluru venues.

Do vendors also charge less for weekday dates?

Often yes, particularly photographers and decorators who are juggling multiple weekend bookings — worth asking directly rather than assuming pricing is fixed regardless of day.
