Runsheet & Event Choreography

The Definitive 3-Day Luxury Destination Wedding Itinerary & Run-Sheet Blueprint

The exact minute-by-minute logistical blueprint we use to coordinate 150+ multi-day luxury weddings across India.

Sunset coastal wedding mandap styled with white orchids and brass temple bells

A flawless 3-day destination wedding requires clockwork precision behind the scenes so that the couple and their families experience only relaxed elegance and joyful memories. Here is the master run-sheet framework developed by the Swariya Weddings team.

Day 1: Arrival, Check-In & Coastal Sundowner / Welcome Soiree

12:00 PM – 04:00 PM: Staggered Airport Pickups & Welcome Desk

Luxury shuttle fleet transfers guests from the airport to the resort. Dedicated hospitality desk conducts express check-ins with customized room keycards and signature welcome hampers.

06:30 PM – 10:30 PM: Sunset Welcome Sundowner / White Party

Acoustic live saxophonist, signature cocktail bars, and artisanal pass-around canapés on the beachfront or garden courtyard. Casual mingling to set the celebration vibe.

Day 2: Vibrant Mehendi, Haldi & High-Energy Sangeet

10:30 AM – 02:00 PM: Poolside Haldi & Carnival Mehendi

Yellow and orange marigold canopies, organic flower petal shower (Phoolon ki Holi), live folk singers, bangle & ittar bars, and live chaat counters.

02:00 PM – 06:30 PM: Structured Rest & Wardrobe Changeover

Essential 4.5-hour buffer for bridal hair & makeup, guest relaxation, and sound checks for the main ballroom stage.

07:30 PM – 01:00 AM+: The Grand Sangeet & Afterparty

Choreographed family dance performances on a custom LED stage, dynamic concert lighting, celebrity DJ set, and premium multi-cuisine buffet.

Day 3: Sacred Muhurtham / Pheras & Grand Gala Reception

08:00 AM – 11:30 AM: Auspicious Vedic Muhurtham / Pheras

Grand Baaraat / Kashi Yatra procession with traditional Nadaswaram or Punjabi dhol, sacred Vedic ceremony under a custom floral mandap, followed by a traditional feast.

07:30 PM – 11:30 PM: Black-Tie Formal Reception Gala

Formal red-carpet bridal entrance, multi-tier cake cutting, champagne toast, live jazz ensemble, and gourmet chef's table dinner.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Swariya manage vendor coordination across all 3 days?

Each event has a designated Swariya Floor Director and Technical Stage Lead who manage vendor arrivals, green-room hospitality, microphone batteries, and timing cues so nothing is left to chance.

What happens in case of unexpected weather or rain during outdoor events?

Every outdoor event has a pre-contracted 'Plan B' indoor ballroom lock with matched floral and acoustic adaptations ready for rapid 45-minute transitions if needed.
