The Ultimate Wedding Checklist: From Engagement to Your Big Day

What to lock in, and when — a month-by-month timeline.

  • Home
  • Blog
  • Ultimate Checklist

This is the timeline we actually use with couples. It's not exhaustive — every wedding has its own specifics — but if you're hitting these milestones roughly on schedule, you're in good shape.

12 months out: The big, hard-to-reverse decisions

  • Set your overall budget, with a 10-15% buffer
  • Agree on a rough guest count with both families
  • Book your venue(s)
  • Book your photographer/videographer
  • Decide: local Bengaluru wedding or destination

9 months out: Locking in the core team

  • Finalize catering and schedule a tasting
  • Book your décor team and agree on a design direction
  • Book entertainment (DJ, live music, performers)
  • Start outfit shopping/fittings

6 months out: Details and logistics

  • Send save-the-dates
  • Finalize the function-by-function schedule (mehndi, sangeet, ceremony, reception)
  • Book makeup and hair artists
  • Arrange guest travel/accommodation if it's a destination wedding
  • Order invitations

3 months out: Confirming everything

  • Send formal invitations
  • Confirm final guest count with caterer and venue
  • Finalize décor mood boards and floor plans
  • Do outfit fittings/alterations
  • Confirm transport and accommodation for out-of-town guests

1 month out: Final details

  • Confirm final headcount with every vendor
  • Create the day-of timeline and share it with all vendors
  • Assign a day-of point of contact who isn't the couple
  • Confirm weather contingency plan for any outdoor functions
  • Final fittings

Week of: Just the essentials

  • Confirm arrival times and setup schedules with every vendor
  • Pack an emergency kit (safety pins, stain remover, extra outfit pieces)
  • Delegate day-of logistics fully — this is not the week to be managing vendor calls yourself

Day of: Just be there

If the earlier steps were handled properly, the day-of checklist should have exactly one item on it: show up and enjoy it. That's the actual goal of everything above.
