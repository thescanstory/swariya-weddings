Destination Weddings from Bengaluru: A Complete Planning Guide

Where couples are going, and what changes about the planning process.

  • Home
  • Blog
  • Destination Weddings Guide

A destination wedding isn't just a local wedding with extra travel — the entire planning approach shifts once guests have to book flights and hotels to attend. Here's what actually changes.

Where couples from Bengaluru are going

Coorg and Chikmagalur are popular for couples wanting a destination feel without the flight — both are a scenic 4-6 hour drive, ideal for weekend-length celebrations with easier guest logistics. Goa remains a strong choice for beach-and-heritage-property combinations. For a bigger production, Jaipur and Udaipur offer palace and heritage venues that Bengaluru's own venue market doesn't have an equivalent for.

Guest travel is the first thing to plan, not the last

Once you set a destination, guest logistics (flights, hotel blocks, local transport) need to be worked out before invitations go out, not after. Blocking hotel rooms early, at group rates, and giving guests a clear window to book travel is the difference between a well-attended destination wedding and a lot of last-minute regrets.

Local vendors vs. bringing your own team

For décor and catering, local vendors at the destination usually make more sense — they know the venue, don't carry travel costs, and can execute faster. For photography and sometimes makeup, many couples prefer bringing a trusted team from Bengaluru for consistency. A good planner sources and vets the local side while coordinating with whichever vendors you bring along.

Legal and ceremonial requirements

Depending on the destination and community, marriage registration requirements can differ from what applies in Bengaluru. This is worth confirming early — sorting out documentation after the wedding is far more painful than checking requirements a few months ahead.

Season and weather

Coorg and Chikmagalur are lush but genuinely wet during monsoon (June-September) — beautiful for photos, risky for outdoor ceremonies without a serious contingency plan. Goa's ideal window is October-March, avoiding both monsoon and peak summer heat. Rajasthan destinations are best October-March as well, since summer heat there is intense.

Budget looks different, not necessarily bigger

Venue costs at some destinations can actually be lower than premium Bengaluru venues — the added cost is almost entirely guest travel, vendor travel, and accommodation. A destination wedding with a smaller guest list can land at a similar total budget to a larger local one.

Where to start

The first real decision isn't the venue — it's an honest guest count, since that drives everything else about feasibility and cost. From there, destination, season, and vendor sourcing fall into place. This is exactly the kind of multi-location coordination we handle end-to-end.
