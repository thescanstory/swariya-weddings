✦ REAL WEDDING CASE STUDY • THE TAMARIND TREE

Ananya & Rohit’s 3-Day Heritage Celebration at The Tamarind Tree

📍 Venue: The Tamarind Tree, Kanakapura Rd 👥 Guest Count: 450 Guests ⏱️ Duration: 3 Days / 4 Functions 💼 Planner: Swariya Weddings

The Brief & Vision

The couple wanted a deep South Indian traditional celebration with Vedic ritual sanctity by the sacred pond, paired with a vibrant, high-energy cocktail sangeet on the sprawling green lawn. With over 200 out-of-town guests traveling from the US, UK, and Mumbai, flawless transportation and guest hospitality were top priorities.

The 3-Day Wedding Schedule Breakdown

Day 1 • Evening

Sangeet & Cocktail Under the Banyan Canopy

4,000+ warm fairy light strands suspended from ancient tree boughs, custom wooden bar staging with craft cocktails, concert-grade acoustic sound, and an LED dance floor seamlessly integrated into the natural grass terrain.

Day 2 • Morning (05:30 AM – 11:30 AM)

Auspicious Muhurtham at the Water Pavilion

Sacred temple mandap built with 800 kg of fresh Madurai jasmine, pink tuberose, and marigold garlands over the reflecting pool. Authentic live Nadaswaram troupe, priest coordination, and hot South Indian filter coffee service for early morning arrivals.

Day 2 • Afternoon

Traditional Plantain Leaf Feast (Brahmin Oota)

Hygienic 3-batch continuous seating in the antique dining hall. Complete traditional 22-dish royal feast managed by Swariya shadow stewards to ensure swift, gracious service.

Day 3 • Evening

Grand Royal Reception & Live Acoustic Ensemble

Cascading floral stage backdrop, gourmet multi-cuisine live stations (Coastal, Chettinad, Pan-Asian & Italian), and a seamless couple entry flanked by cold-spark pyrotechnics.

Budget Allocation & Zero-Commission Savings

By operating on Swariya's flat management fee model rather than taking 15–20% vendor markups, the family saved an estimated ₹6.8 Lakhs on third-party vendor contracts.
