✦ REAL DESTINATION CELEBRATION ✦

200-Guest Coastal Luxury at Alila Diwa Goa

From Sunset Welcome Sundowner to Courtyard Pheras: Complete Logistical Timeline, Infinity Pool Decor & Zero-Commission Investment Breakdown.

The Couple's Vision & Challenge

Rohan (San Francisco, CA) and Sneha (Bengaluru) envisioned a breezy, sophisticated coastal destination wedding for 200 close friends and family flying in from North America, the UK, and across India. They wanted five distinct aesthetic experiences over 3 days while ensuring zero stress on out-of-town guest logistics.

Event Snapshot at a Glance

  • 📍 Property: Alila Diwa South Goa (Majorda, Goa)
  • 👥 Guest Count: 200 Guests (85 Room Block at The Diwa Wing)
  • ⏱️ Duration: 3 Days / 5 Signature Functions
  • 🌸 Aesthetic: Coastal Pampas Grass, White Orchids, Brass Oil Torans, and Tropical Foliage
  • 💰 Total Investment: ₹68,50,000 (100% Itemized with 0% Vendor Markups)

3-Day Master Runsheet & Production Execution

Day 1: Arrival Hospitality & Bohemian Sundowner

Dedicated charter shuttles from Goa Dabolim (GOI) and Mopa (GOX) airports. Evening Sunset Sundowner at the Courtyard overlooking paddy fields with acoustic guitarist, live wood-fired pizza ovens, and artisanal feni cocktails.

Day 2: Infinity Pool Mehendi & The Grand Sangeet

Morning pool party with floating flamingo lounges, live percussionists, and coconut water bar. Evening Sangeet on a custom 40-foot LED stage with synchronized concert intelligent lighting in the Grand Ballroom.

Day 3: Sacred Sunset Pheras & Black-Tie Reception

Vintage convertible Baaraat arrival under Goa palms. Mandap set atop the infinity pool deck with imported baby's breath and fresh lotus blooms. Concluded with a 5-course coastal seafood banquet and jazz quartet.
