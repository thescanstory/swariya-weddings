✦ REAL WEDDING CASE STUDY • AMITA RASA

Sneha & Vikram’s Eco-Luxury Temple Wedding at Amita Rasa

📍 Venue: Amita Rasa, Nandi Foothills 👥 Guest Count: 300 Guests ⏱️ Duration: 2 Days / 3 Functions 💼 Planner: Swariya Weddings

The Aesthetic Concept

Sneha and Vikram wanted a serene destination-feel wedding against the dramatic backdrop of Nandi Hills. The priority was sustainable decor: zero single-use plastics, locally sourced temple flowers (marigolds, brassica, tuberoses), brass artisanal lamps, and an authentic multi-course vegetarian culinary journey.

Event Highlights & Production Details

Function 1 • Sunset Haldi & Mehendi

Open-Air Courtyard Carnival

Earthy cane furniture, marigold canopies, terracotta water urns for flower petal showers, and live folk percussionists as guests arrived in customized chartered buses from Central Bengaluru.

Function 2 • Morning Muhurtham

Granite Mandap & Vedic Ceremony

Constructed in harmony with Amita Rasa’s carved stone architecture. Over 500 brass diyas, authentic temple bells, and a shaded pergola for senior family members with misting fans for outdoor comfort.

Function 3 • Twilight Reception

Amphitheatre Lawn & Starlit Dinner

Bespoke low-emission ambient lighting illuminating the rocky hill contours, live Carnatic-Western fusion band, and curated regional dining stations with customized cocktail pairings.
