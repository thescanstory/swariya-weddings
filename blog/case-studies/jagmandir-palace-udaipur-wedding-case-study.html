✦ REAL ROYAL PALACE CELEBRATION ✦

250-Guest Royal Splendor at Jagmandir Island Palace

Boat Convoy Logistics, Freestanding Heritage Floral Mandap & Zero-Markup Royal Investment Blueprint on Lake Pichola, Udaipur.

The Palace Blueprint & Operational Challenge

Hosting a grand wedding on an island palace requires extraordinary logistical discipline. Every floral stem, generator, sound mixer, and guest must be ferried across Lake Pichola. Swariya Weddings orchestrated seamless waterborne production schedules while adhering to strict Mewar heritage conservation standards.

Palace Event Metrics

  • 📍 Venue: Jagmandir Island Palace, Lake Pichola (Udaipur, Rajasthan)
  • 👥 Guest Count: 250 Guests
  • 🚤 Water Logistics: 6-Boat Continuous Shuttle Ferry Fleet
  • 👑 Highlights: Royal Elephant Stone Pavilion, 1,000 Brass Diyas & Lake Sunset Pheras
  • 💰 Total Investment: ₹92,00,000 (Direct-to-Vendor Open Book Accounting)

Logistical Execution & Waterway Transfers

Production load-in commenced 36 hours prior via specialized pontoon barges. On event day, 250 guests boarded decorated royal boats at Rameshwar Ghat, greeted with chilled scented towels and live flute melodies during the 10-minute water cruise.

The Mandap was assembled in the central marble courtyard beneath ancient neem trees, styled with 500 kg of fresh local marigold and tuberose garlands cascading into brass water vessels.
