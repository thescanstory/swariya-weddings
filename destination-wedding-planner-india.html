Frequently Asked Questions

What destinations in India does Swariya Weddings plan weddings for? −

Swariya Weddings plans luxury destination weddings across all major Indian regions, including Goa (beachfront resorts and Portuguese villas), Rajasthan (Udaipur, Jaipur, Jodhpur palace weddings), Kerala (Kumarakom backwaters and Fort Kochi heritage estates), Karnataka Hill Stations (Coorg coffee estates and Chikmagalur), South India heritage hubs (Mahabalipuram, Hyderabad), and North Indian hill retreats (Mussoorie, Jim Corbett).

How does Swariya manage destination wedding logistics and vendor quality outside Bengaluru? +

Swariya combines trusted local destination vendor relationships (catering, sound, transport) with our in-house master production leads (carpentry, floral styling, lighting directors) dispatched directly to the venue. This guarantees premium aesthetic standards without excessive destination markups.

What is the average cost of a destination wedding in India with Swariya Weddings? +

Destination weddings in India typically range from ₹35 Lakhs to ₹1.5 Crores+ depending on the destination (e.g. Coorg vs. Udaipur Palace), guest count (usually 100 to 250 guests), and resort room inventory. Swariya operates on a 100% transparent zero-markup policy where couples receive wholesale trade rates directly.

Can NRI couples plan their destination wedding in India remotely? +

Yes. Over 40% of Swariya's couples reside in the US, UK, UAE, and Singapore. We provide time-zone aligned virtual consultations, 3D architectural renders, digital cloud budgeting ledgers, and end-to-end guest RSVP and airport transfer management.
