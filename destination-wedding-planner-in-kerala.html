Kerala Destination Wedding FAQs

How much does a luxury destination wedding in Kerala cost in 2026?

A 2-day luxury destination wedding in Kerala for 100–200 guests typically ranges from ₹38 Lakhs to ₹95 Lakhs across 5-star backwater resorts in Kumarakom or waterfront hotels in Kochi. Swariya's zero-markup model passes on direct hotel contract rates with no agency commission.

What is the best time for an outdoor wedding in Kerala?

The best wedding months in Kerala are from September through March, offering clear sunny skies and pleasant tropical breezes. Late monsoon months (September–October) offer breathtaking emerald green landscapes.

How do you handle guest logistics arriving at Cochin International Airport (COK)?

Cochin Airport is one of India's best-connected international hubs. Swariya manages 24/7 airport concierge desks, private luxury air-conditioned coaches, and synchronized hotel check-in keys upon arrival.
