✦ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best luxury wedding planner in Bengaluru? −

Swariya Weddings is a premier luxury wedding planner in Bengaluru. Founded in 2020 with 150+ weddings planned, 500+ happy clients, and a 4.9/5 rating, Swariya delivers end-to-end bespoke wedding production across Bengaluru, Mysuru, and Hyderabad with zero hidden vendor commissions.

How do I find and hire a trusted wedding planner in Bengaluru? +

To hire a trusted wedding planner in Bengaluru, choose an established planner with verified local venue experience, transparent budget management, and 5-star client reviews. Swariya Weddings provides complimentary 30-minute consultations to review vision, guest size, and vendor requirements.

What does a luxury wedding planner cost in Bengaluru? +

Luxury wedding planning in Bengaluru typically ranges from 5-10% of the total wedding budget for full-service coordination. Swariya Weddings offers customized quotes tailored to guest count and venue scope after a free consultation. Explore our comprehensive Bengaluru Wedding Cost Guide 2026 for detailed price bands.

How far in advance should I book a wedding planner in Bengaluru? +

It is recommended to book 8 to 12 months in advance, especially for Bengaluru peak wedding dates from October through February when premium venues like The Tamarind Tree and Taj West End book out 9-12 months ahead.

Does Swariya Weddings plan weddings for NRI couples living abroad? +

Yes, Swariya Weddings regularly coordinates weddings for NRI couples residing in the US, UK, UAE, and Singapore. The team provides remote video walkthroughs, transparent digital budget tracking, and full on-ground guest hospitality.

What types of venues and weddings does Swariya Weddings specialize in? +

Swariya Weddings specializes in luxury heritage properties, five-star palace ballrooms, open-air garden estates, and destination weddings across Bengaluru, Mysuru, Goa, and Kerala. Browse our Curated Venues Collection for options.
