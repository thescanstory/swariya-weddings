How much does a coffee plantation wedding in Coorg cost in 2026?

A 2-day intimate luxury wedding in Coorg or Chikmagalur for 100–180 guests typically ranges from ₹30 Lakhs to ₹75 Lakhs. This covers resort buyouts/room blocks, misty outdoor floral mandaps, curated multi-cuisine dining, sound equipment, and full-service coordination. What is the best time of year for an outdoor wedding in Coorg?

The ideal season is from October to April, when the weather is pleasantly cool with clear starry nights and misty mornings. November through February offers the most crisp hill-station climate.
