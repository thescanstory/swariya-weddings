Featured Editorial Story Angles

1. The Fiduciary Disruption: Replacing Hidden 20%–30% Commissions with Direct Trade Pricing

For decades, the Indian wedding planning sector has operated on undisclosed vendor markups—charging commissions on catering, floristry, lighting, and sound that often inflate couple budgets by 15% to 30%. Swariya Weddings is championing a 100% fiduciary standard: couples pay a transparent flat management fee, and all vendor invoices are passed through at actual trade procurement rates.

"When a wedding planner takes kickbacks from florists or caterers, their loyalty shifts away from the couple. By working on a strict 0% vendor markup model, our clients audit every single line item and save up to 25% on luxury setups." — Swariya Weddings Editorial Team

2. Ephemeral Architecture: How Photorealistic 3D Spatial CAD Previews are Eliminating Wedding Day Anxiety

Traditional wedding planning relies heavily on Pinterest mood boards that frequently fail to translate to the actual venue ballroom or lawn.
