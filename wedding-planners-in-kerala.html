Frequently Asked Questions — Kerala Weddings

What is the cost of a backwater destination wedding in Kumarakom / Alleppey? +

A 2 to 3-day luxury backwater wedding in Kerala for 80-150 guests generally ranges from ₹25 Lakhs to ₹60 Lakhs including luxury resort cottage stays, houseboat cruises, and authentic Sadya catering.

Can we arrange a houseboat pre-wedding sunset cruise for our guests? +

Yes! Swariya arranges customized luxury double-decker houseboats with live Kerala musicians, tender coconut bars, and traditional coastal appetizers.

Do you coordinate Kerala Christian (Syrian Catholic / Orthodox) and Nair ceremonies? +

Yes, we manage Manthrakodi rituals, Minnu tying, church choirs, and traditional Nair Thalikettu ceremonies with authentic Nilavilakku setups.
