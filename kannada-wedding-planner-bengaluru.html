The Sacred Ritual Sequence

We ensure every ceremony adheres to family customs and purohit requirements while ensuring seamless guest comfort:

1. Nandi Puja & Dev Karya

Invoking divine blessings for an obstacle-free wedding, setup with traditional brass Kalash, fresh banana saplings, and ceremonial flower garlands.

2. Kaashi Yatre & Varapooje

The groom's ceremonial pilgrimage procession, received with silk umbrella, walking stick, and welcoming rituals by the bride's father.

3. Dhareheradu (Kanyadaana)

The solemn moment where the bride's parents place her hand in the groom's over sacred milk and holy water poured through a ceremonial conch.

4. Mangalasutra & Saptapadi

The tying of the sacred Maangalya accompanied by classical Nadaswaram crescendos, followed by the seven sacred vows around the Agni kunda.

5. Traditional Hasemane & Oota

Authentic plantain leaf banqueting with authentic Karnataka Brahmin and vegetarian delicacies, coordinated for rapid, hygienic guest dining turns.

6. Grihapravesha & Reception

Welcoming the new bride to her marital home with rice-pot overturning, followed by an evening celebration with live fusion music and grand stage setups.
